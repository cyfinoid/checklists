Build vs Buy — AI-era Security Decision Matrix

Walk one proposed control or purchase through the decision gates, score it against the five test dimensions, and get a defensible outcome: Buy · Build · Fix / consolidate · Defer (do nothing yet).

00Candidate

01Decision gates

Is the risk or delay you are fixing material today?

Frightening demos are not exposure. If it is not material, the framework is allowed to stop here.

Does an existing control mostly cover it?
Is it a narrow workflow you can own and maintain?
Will it deliver a measurable reduction in exposure or latency?

If you cannot say how you will measure the improvement, the buying conversation is premature.

02Five test dimensions

Threat relevance

Does it address a threat path attackers actually use against your exposure?

Latency / exposure reduction

Which of know → decide → act → verify → recover does it actually shorten?

Authority & blast radius

What can it execute, which identities does it inherit, how fast can you revoke it?

Auditability & operational burden

Can actions be reconstructed? Who operates it at 2 AM?

Dependency & exit cost

What happens when the provider changes pricing, model or behaviour?
